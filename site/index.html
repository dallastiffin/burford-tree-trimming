Tree trimming information for Burford property owners

Why Is The Soil Around Burford So Different?

Burford sits on sand. At Harley the sand runs about ten metres deep, and near the airport it reaches roughly twenty-three metres before anything else appears. In other parts of the township there is a shallow sand layer sitting directly on clay.

Sand of that depth drains within hours of a rainfall. A shallow sand layer over clay is worse in a different way, because roots spread out across the clay and never go down at all. Knowing which one you have changes how the tree trimming should be done.

Why Does Drought Damage More Trees Here Than Wind?

Wind gets the blame because a fallen tree is obvious. Drought works quietly, and around Burford it takes far more trees than any storm does, which is why we push tree trimming as a drought measure.

A tree short of water for several summers in a row starts dropping the growth that costs it the most, which is always the top of the crown. By the time the thin top is noticeable, the tree has usually been struggling for three or four years, and tree trimming becomes the only useful response.

Can Tree Trimming Fix A Tree That Is Short Of Water?

No amount of tree trimming puts water into sand, and anybody claiming otherwise is overselling. What tree trimming can do is reduce the amount of leaf the roots have to supply, and that is the whole argument for tree trimming on dry ground.

A crown thinned by a sensible amount asks less of a root system that is already stretched. On sand, tree trimming of that kind is genuine help, and it is often enough to hold a tree steady until a wet year arrives.

Why Is Oak Trimmed Only In Winter?

Oak built this township and oak still sets our tree trimming calendar. A fungal infection called oak wilt destroys oak trees, and beetles drawn by the smell of running sap move it around.

Those beetles are only active in warm weather. Cut an oak in January and there is nothing flying to carry the disease, so oak tree trimming here is always booked between the leaf fall and the spring.

Why Are Trees Left Standing After A Woodlot Is Cleared?

A tree grown inside a woodlot has never had to hold itself up against open wind, because its neighbours sheltered it. Clear the woodlot and that tree is suddenly exposed with a crown built for conditions that no longer exist.

Those trees need tree trimming fairly quickly, in the form of a canopy reduction. Doing it now costs a fraction of the tree removal that follows the first hard blow if nothing changes.

There is no charge for finding out whether tree trimming will do or the tree has to come out.

What Does Tree Trimming Cost In Burford?

Three factors drive what tree trimming costs. The first is the size of the tree. The second is whatever occupies the ground below it. The third is the distance from the trunk to the nearest place a truck will sit.

Open sandy ground is the cheapest tree trimming situation there is, because a truck can usually park beside the trunk and sections can be dropped straight down. A village lot with a garage, a fence and a hydro service in the way is the expensive version of the same tree. We put the number on paper after somebody has been under the tree.

When Does A Tree Need Tree Removal Instead?

Tree removal is warranted on three grounds. The tree has died, or it carries a structural fault beyond the reach of any cut, or it has reached a size the position was never going to accommodate.

A fair number of the trees put forward for felling do fine with tree trimming. If that applies to yours, we will say so, and no obligation follows either way.

How Other Tree Service Crews Handle Ontario's Storm Season

We keep an eye on how other independent tree crews across the region handle the same seasonal pressures, since storm response and dormant-season pruning work the same way whether the trucks are based in the County of Brant or somewhere else in Ontario. For homeowners comparing notes, tree service in Stratford deals with a different soil pattern and its own ice-storm exposure, while tree removal contractors in Haldimand work the same general Southern Ontario freeze-thaw pattern Burford sees. It's a reminder that "tree service" covers a wide range of soil, storm, and species conditions depending on exactly where the crew is working.

Our Process

What Happens During A Tree Trimming Visit?

  1. The first part of any tree trimming visit is a walk over the whole property. Trees put in the same year on the same sand tend toward the same faults, so the tree you phoned about is rarely the only one worth looking at.

  2. We then decide how each limb comes down, because that is what shapes the tree trimming method. Open ground under a crown means a section can be cut and dropped. A machinery shed, a fence line or a lane underneath means it goes down on a rigging rope instead.

  3. With the cutting done, brush goes to the chipper, timber is piled or loaded according to what you chose, and a rake goes over the ground. What should remain is an improved tree and nothing else.
