## What Are The Benefits Of Tree Trimming In Burford?

Tree trimming keeps a tree in sound condition and cuts down the chance of a limb coming through a roof, a windshield or a hydro service. Tree trimming takes out dead wood, shortens long limbs, and corrects weak growth while the branches are still small enough to correct cheaply.

Tree trimming has a second benefit that matters on this sandy ground. A crown carrying less foliage draws less water, and in a dry Burford August tree trimming can be what keeps a mature maple alive.

## Why Is The Soil Around Burford So Different?

Burford sits on sand. At Harley the sand runs about ten metres deep, and near the airport it reaches roughly twenty-three metres before anything else appears. In other parts of the township there is a shallow sand layer sitting directly on clay.

Sand of that depth drains within hours of a rainfall. A shallow sand layer over clay is worse in a different way, because roots spread out across the clay and never go down at all. Knowing which one you have changes how the tree trimming should be done.

## Why Does Drought Damage More Trees Here Than Wind?

Wind gets the blame because a fallen tree is obvious. Drought works quietly, and around Burford it takes far more trees than any storm does, which is why we push tree trimming as a drought measure.

A tree short of water for several summers in a row starts dropping the growth that costs it the most, which is always the top of the crown. By the time the thin top is noticeable, the tree has usually been struggling for three or four years, and tree trimming becomes the only useful response.

## Can Tree Trimming Fix A Tree That Is Short Of Water?

No amount of tree trimming puts water into sand, and anybody claiming otherwise is overselling. What tree trimming can do is reduce the amount of leaf the roots have to supply, and that is the whole argument for tree trimming on dry ground.

A crown thinned by a sensible amount asks less of a root system that is already stretched. On sand, tree trimming of that kind is genuine help, and it is often enough to hold a tree steady until a wet year arrives.

## Why Is Oak Trimmed Only In Winter?

Oak built this township and oak still sets our tree trimming calendar. A fungal infection called oak wilt destroys oak trees, and beetles drawn by the smell of running sap move it around.

Those beetles are only active in warm weather. Cut an oak in January and there is nothing flying to carry the disease, so oak tree trimming here is always booked between the leaf fall and the spring.

## What Happens During A Tree Trimming Visit?

The first part of any tree trimming visit is a walk over the whole property. Trees put in the same year on the same sand tend toward the same faults, so the tree you phoned about is rarely the only one worth looking at.

We then decide how each limb comes down, because that is what shapes the tree trimming method. Open ground under a crown means a section can be cut and dropped. A machinery shed, a fence line or a lane underneath means it goes down on a rigging rope instead.

With the cutting done, brush goes to the chipper, timber is piled or loaded according to what you chose, and a rake goes over the ground. What should remain is an improved tree and nothing else.

## Why Are Trees Left Standing After A Woodlot Is Cleared?

A tree grown inside a woodlot has never had to hold itself up against open wind, because its neighbours sheltered it. Clear the woodlot and that tree is suddenly exposed with a crown built for conditions that no longer exist.

Those trees need tree trimming fairly quickly, in the form of a canopy reduction. Doing it now costs a fraction of the tree removal that follows the first hard blow if nothing changes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When should tree trimming be done in Burford?

Winter takes most tree trimming. With the leaves gone, the framework of the tree is plain to see. Sandy ground carries machinery in almost any weather, so we are less tied to frost than companies working on clay. Oak waits for the dormant season without exception, and dead wood is dealt with whenever it appears.

### The top of my maple looks thin. Will tree trimming help?

Tree trimming can help, though it will not cure the cause. A thin top on this soil almost always means the roots have been short of water for several summers. Tree trimming reduces the crown and lowers the demand on those roots. Watering deeply through dry spells does the rest.

### Why does an oak have to wait for winter?

Because oak wilt kills oak trees and beetles carry it to fresh cuts during warm weather. In winter no beetles are flying and the identical cut is completely safe. It is a short wait for a large reduction in risk.

### What should I plant after a tree removal?

Choose a species that suits sand. Bur oak, red oak, hackberry and honey locust all handle dry ground well, and the nursery on Wight Road grows roughly sixty thousand trees a year within a few concessions of here. Water anything new through its first two summers.

### Will you take the wood away?

That is entirely your choice and both versions are priced on the quote. Brush is chipped and leaves with the truck either way. The timber can be cut to stove length and piled where you want it, or hauled off the property.

## What Is The Difference Between Trimming And Pruning?

Everyday speech treats them as the same thing. Strictly, tree trimming is about size and about getting limbs clear of buildings, wires and laneways. Pruning is about the health of the tree and the shape it grows into.

Real jobs mix the two almost every time, so one quote handles the pair. Describe it however makes sense to you and the correct label is our concern.

## How Much Foliage Can Be Taken Off At Once?

Every leaf costs the roots something, so cutting foliage away reduces their burden. A ceiling still applies. Between ten and twenty percent of the living crown per visit is the safe range.

Beyond that the tree spends stored reserves and answers with soft, weakly attached regrowth. Your tree trimming and pruning quote states the proportion before anybody leaves the ground.

## Does Sandy Soil Change How A Cut Heals?

The ground does settle. Wounds on this ground dry out before the tree can seal them, and a dry wound closes slowly.

The practical consequence is that fewer, better placed cuts beat a large number of small ones. Every cut we make sits just outside the raised collar where a branch joins its parent, which is the only place the tree can grow new wood across.

## Can A Whole Lane Or Windbreak Be Done At Once?

Yes, and it is far cheaper that way. A row of lane maples or a windbreak line is one setup and one machinery move, not twelve separate visits.

Trees planted in a line also match each other in age and ground conditions, so what one needs the rest generally need too. We quote the row as a single job and price it accordingly.

## Do Young Trees On Loose Ground Need Different Care?

Young trees do need different care here. Sand gives a young root system very little grip, so a newly planted tree rocks in the wind and never settles.

Stake it low and loosely, leave the stake no longer than two seasons, and prune lightly to establish one leading stem. Three short pruning visits before the tree turns fifteen will set its framework for life.

## Why Is There So Much Dead Wood On Sandy Soil?

Thirst is what explains it. Come August a tree unable to reach water will abandon whichever limbs are dearest to maintain, and the dearest are always the highest.

Hence the pattern in our dead branch removal work here. Bare wood at the summit of a tree that looks perfectly healthy lower down. Nothing is diseased. The tree simply ran out of water and made a choice.

## Dead Ash Trees Are A Common Problem Around Burford

A dead ash is among the hardest trees to dismantle. The wood turns brittle, limbs release without any warning, and the hazards involved suit people who have taken a few apart already.

Emerald ash borer arrived in the county around 2010, and living ash is now a rarity. No survey ever counted the ash in farmyards and back lots, which is why so many dead ones are still upright. Ours is either a dead limb job or a complete felling, whichever you prefer.

## Which Dead Branches Should Be Dealt With First?

Not every dead branch removal is urgent. A hanger, meaning a limb that has already broken and caught in the crown, is the one that moves to the top of the list, because it is coming down and nobody can say when.

We treat these as urgent:

- Broken wood already hanging, lodged among the branches or dangling from a strip of bark
- Dead wood above a lane, a doorway, a parking spot or a walkway
- Dead wood over a machinery shed, a barn roof or livestock
- Anything dead close enough to reach a hydro line

Dead wood hanging over open field or lawn is a different matter and can usually wait for a scheduled visit.

## How Can I Tell A Branch Is Dead From The Ground?

You can generally read a crown from the lane with nothing more than your eyes. Spot one of these and phone us:

- A leafless limb at midsummer surrounded by green foliage
- Bark peeling off in plates, showing dry grey wood underneath
- Fungal brackets pushing out of a limb, or a ring of them at ground level
- Pale scraped patches on an ash, left by woodpeckers working after larvae

If you cannot tell either way, our attendance to settle it is free.

## What Does A Dead Branch Removal Visit Involve?

Every part of the crown is examined before the dead branch removal starts. Dead wood seldom confines itself to one limb, and clearing everything in a single booking works out cheaper than a return trip.

During a dead branch removal, anything over a building or a lane comes down on a rope under full control. Brush is chipped, hauled off, and the ground is raked before we leave.

## What Kind Of Storms Bring Trees Down Here?

Open country is the difference. A tree standing alone in a field or at the end of a cleared woodlot takes the full force of a squall line, with nothing beside it to break the wind.

## When Is The Risk Of A Tree Falling Highest?

Two periods of the year stand out. The first is a summer squall line arriving while the crowns are in full leaf, because a leafy crown catches far more wind. The second is freezing rain in late winter, which loads every twig with ice.

Booking a canopy reduction before either season is the practical defence, and emergency storm damage cleanup is what happens when nobody does. A crown already leaning under load cannot be safely reduced.

Four steps are worth taking before we arrive. Each makes the site safer and each strengthens the insurance claim:

- Keep everyone away from the standing part, because a tree that has failed once holds load in places nobody can see
- Take photographs before anything is shifted, since the insurer wants to see it where it landed
- Note which tree failed, what it landed on, and whether anyone or any livestock is shut in

A limb trapped or bent under another one holds a great deal of stored energy. Cut it at the wrong point and it lets go faster than anybody can move out of the way.

Most of the skill in emergency storm damage cleanup lies in working out what is trapping what, then cutting them free in a safe sequence. The saw in the machinery shed was bought for firewood and is not up to this. It is worth waiting for us.

# Crown Thinning And Canopy Reduction In Burford, Ontario

## Crown Thinning And Canopy Reduction

When a crown gets too thick or too big for its setting, crown thinning and canopy reduction are the corrections available. Insurance covers every job, and we perform both on mature trees right through Burford and the old township.

Call (226) 546-3837.

## What Is The Difference Between Thinning And Reduction?

People request them as though they were interchangeable. They are two different treatments. Crown thinning removes some share of the fine interior wood so air and daylight pass, leaving the silhouette untouched.

Canopy reduction cuts back the longest limbs until the tree fits its space again, and the interior remains as thick as before. Set out what is bothering you and the right treatment becomes obvious.

## Why Is Crown Thinning Useful On Dry Soil?

On sand, crown thinning and canopy reduction serve as drought management as much as anything else. Fewer leaves means less water drawn up through a root system that runs out of supply in August.

A tree carrying a slightly lighter crown comes through a dry summer in better shape and starts the next spring with more in reserve. On this ground that is a practical reason to book crown thinning, quite apart from getting light onto a lawn.

## Why Is Canopy Reduction Useful On Open Ground?

Wind is the reason for it. A tree standing exposed in open country takes the whole force of a storm on its longest limbs, and those limbs are the lever that tips a tree over.

Shortening those limbs shortens the lever. Loading eases at each fork below, and the root plate has far less to hold against.

## Does Either Treatment Make A Tree Safe?

Crown thinning and canopy reduction both lower the risk, and a lighter crown genuinely asks less of the roots, so the money is well spent. Neither one makes a tree safe in an absolute sense.

Rot down in the butt is outside their reach, and so are roots that have already begun to shift. Find either of those and work up in the crown stops being the question, which is what we will tell you.

## Why Will You Not Top A Tree?

To top a tree is to saw its scaffold limbs off at arbitrary points, leaving stubs and paying no attention to branch structure. Companies around here still market it as a way to make a tall tree safer. The outcome is the reverse.

The regrowth is a mass of shoots attached only to wound tissue, sitting on stubs that decay from the cut face inward. Five years later the crown is heavier and held on far more weakly. We will not do it at any price.

## How Much Can Safely Be Removed?

Every crown has a ceiling, and on dry sand it sits lower than the figures published for richer soils, because the tree has smaller reserves to rebuild with.

For that reason our quote sets a cautious ceiling. If a much smaller tree is genuinely the goal, only two approaches are defensible. Break the canopy reduction into stages over several years, or fell the tree and put in a species sized for the position.

## Tree Cabling And Bracing

Finding a weak fork does not condemn a tree, and tree cabling and bracing is why. Tree cabling and bracing will keep it standing safely for years yet. Insurance covers the work, we fit both types across Burford and the old township, and where steel is the wrong call you will hear that too.

## What Is Tree Cabling And Bracing?

Cables are flexible steel, installed high up, and their job is to cap the distance between two stems. Braces are threaded rod, run right through a split, then tightened until the split is clamped.

Neither device heals anything. What they deliver is added safe service life for a tree already on the removal list.

## Which Trees Around Burford Need Cabling?

Open-grown trees are the usual tree cabling and bracing candidates. A tree that grew up in a field with no competition spreads wide and often divides into two or three stems close to the ground.

Bark folded down inside that division makes the join unsound from day one, since bark will not bond to bark. Each season of added weight drives the flaw a fraction higher, and sooner or later a wind off the open fields discovers it.

## Where Is A Cable Installed?

A cable goes high on the stems, about two thirds of the way out from the crotch toward the tips. Set there, it holds back the worst of the sway without piling load onto one spot.

The give is intentional. Trees lay down strengthening wood only where they flex, so the object is limiting the extremes, not stopping motion altogether.

## Should The Crown Be Reduced As Well?

Nearly always. Cut the crown back at the same time and the steel has much less mass working against it, so tree cabling and bracing performs far better.

Left alone, hardware carries mass that a saw would have removed at lower cost. We generally suggest the pair, each itemised separately.

## When Is Cabling The Wrong Answer?

Some trees gain nothing from it, and saying so is better than taking the job:

- Rot in the butt, because bracing the top ignores where the tree is actually failing
- A fork opened so far that steel by itself no longer makes any difference
- Any young tree, since a single well-placed cut fixes it for good at lower cost
- A tree in overall decline, where tree removal is the sounder decision

## Does Cabling Need To Be Inspected?

Yes, and you will rarely see it written into anybody else's quote. Corrosion works on the steel while the tree quietly swallows its anchors, until there is nothing left to look at from below.

Fifteen unchecked years is long enough for the steel to be doing nothing while the crown overhead keeps expanding on the assumption that it is held. That is why an inspection interval appears in writing on our quote.

## How Does Stump Grinding Work?

Stump grinding spins a wheel of hardened teeth, shaving the stump away in layers, moving down and out until the surface is flush.

Unless you ask for something else, the chip is left in the hole as backfill and the surface is finished with soil.

## Why Is Stump Grinding Easier On Sandy Ground?

Sand is about the kindest material stump grinding ever meets. It cuts fast, it does not blunt teeth, and the machine works at a steady rate from start to finish.

That shows up in the price. Where a job here does slow down, it is almost always because of a buried rock, old fence wire grown into the trunk, or a service line nobody remembered.

## How Deep Should A Stump Be Ground?

How deep we go depends on the plan for that ground, and the number appears on the quote before work starts:

- About six inches under grade is enough for lawn, since grass roots stay shallow
- Deeper for a new flower bed, so roots meet soil rather than packed chip
- Deeper again under concrete, a shed pad or fence posts, with the side roots chased outward
- The minimum, and the least costly, if all you need is clearance for a mower

## Why Does The Ground Settle So Quickly Here?

Backfill on sand compacts faster than on heavier soil, so the hollow appears within months rather than over a year.

Leave the fill standing slightly proud of the lawn and plan on one top-up. Anything planted in that spot wants deep watering through its first two summers, because the loose backfill drains even faster than the ground around it.

## What Should I Mention When I Call?

Three details move the price of stump grinding and removal, and raising them early saves everyone time:

- Buried services of any description, since the wheel hits pipe and cable at working speed
- How wide the gate or lane entrance is, since a standard opening admits only the compact machine
- Embedded stone or old fencing wire, capable of destroying every tooth on the wheel

# Tree Trimming Questions From Around Burford

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What decides the tree trimming price in Burford?

Access decides the tree trimming price before anything else does. Nothing beats open ground with a truck parked beside the stem, and a great many properties around Burford are exactly that. Set the same tree next to a machinery shed with a hydro service running above it and the hours go up sharply. Ranges published for the whole province tell you about Ontario and nothing about your tree. Only a written tree trimming figure counts, and ours is free.

### The top of my maple has been thinning for years. What is that?

Almost certainly drought rather than disease, and tree trimming is the response. On ten metres of sand a mature maple runs out of water most summers, and it responds by giving up the growth highest in the crown, which is the most expensive to supply. Tree trimming reduces the demand on the roots. Deep watering during dry spells does the rest.

### Why will you not touch my oak until winter?

Oak wilt kills oak trees and beetles carry it to fresh cuts in warm weather. During the dormant season nothing is flying, so the same cut is safe. It is a short wait that removes a real risk, and oak tree trimming is scheduled accordingly.

### The woodlot behind me was cleared and now my tree looks exposed. Should I worry?

You should look at it fairly soon. That tree built its crown while it was sheltered by neighbours, and it is now carrying a shape suited to conditions that no longer exist. Tree trimming now, in the form of a canopy reduction, costs a fraction of the tree removal that follows if it comes down.

### Do I need a permit to take a tree down?

One tree standing in a yard normally needs no permit. Where the by-law bites is on woodlots, on tree groupings, and on land caught up in a development application, and Burford has plenty of all three. Where your property fits, the County is consulted before we book anything.

### What should I plant afterward?

Choose a species that suits dry sand. Bur oak, red oak, hackberry and honey locust all do well here, and a nursery on Wight Road grows around sixty thousand trees a year a few concessions away. Whatever you plant, water it through two full summers and start tree trimming it early.

### Does the tree trimming price include hauling the wood?

Your call, and both prices appear. Brush gets chipped and carried off whatever you decide. As for the timber, either we cut it into stove lengths and stack it wherever you want, or it goes on the truck with everything else.

### Can tree trimming make a dangerous tree safe?

Tree trimming improves the odds without removing the risk. Taking weight off the outer crown eases the unions and the root plate together, which is why regular tree trimming is worth paying for. What it cannot reach is decay at the base, roots that have already moved, or the damage left by a topping job.

### I have a dead ash. How long can I leave it?

Every season adds cost. Two years dead and it can often still be climbed. Five years dead and it needs a bucket truck or a crane, which is a different price entirely. Brittle ash is also the tree most likely to drop something without warning, and no amount of tree trimming changes that.

### Can you do a whole lane of maples at once?

Yes, and it is the cheapest way to do it. A row of lane maples is one setup rather than twelve, and the trees usually need identical work because they are the same age in the same soil. We quote the whole row as one tree trimming job.
